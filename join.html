How to join

Help us learn more about heart failure.

HeartShare is a research study about heart failure across the United States. Anyone age 30 or older can join, with or without heart failure.

What is HFpEF? HeartShare focuses on a type of heart failure called heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, or HFpEF. In HFpEF, the heart pumps normally, but it is too stiff to fill with blood the way it should.

How participation works

Everyone starts in the Registry. Some people in the Registry may then be able to join the Deep Phenotyping Cohort.

Step 1 · Everyone

The HeartShare Registry

You answer online surveys about your health and daily life. You can do them from home, on your phone or computer, when it works for you.

What is involved

  • The first set of surveys takes about 30 to 45 minutes.
  • After that, monthly surveys take about 5 to 10 minutes.
  • No in-person visits.

Who can join

  • Adults age 30 or older.
  • People who have been diagnosed with heart failure.
  • People who have not been diagnosed with heart failure. They help us compare.
  • You need to be able to fill out surveys online.

Payment

$50
Each quarter, we hold a gift card drawing for Registry participants. Some people win a $50 gift card.
Step 2 · Some Registry participants

Deep Phenotyping Cohort

“Deep phenotyping” means taking a closer, more detailed look at your health. This part of the study takes place in person at one of our partner medical centers.

What is involved

  • A first visit over two days, with detailed health exams.
  • Blood samples and other tests.
  • Optional: a small tissue sample (biopsy). The study team will explain what this involves before you decide.
  • One follow-up visit about one year after your first visit. More visits may be added in the future.

Who can join

  • People who are already in the Registry.
  • Adults age 30 or older with HFpEF.
  • You need to be able to visit one of our partner medical centers.
  • You must meet other study requirements. The study team will go over these with you.

How to get started

Joining is simple. Here is what to expect.

  1. Contact us

    Email heartsharestudy@northwestern.edu, call 312-503-1203, or use our contact form.

  2. Learn about the study

    We will send you information about what taking part involves and answer your questions.

  3. Give your consent

    Read the consent form at your own pace. Ask any questions you have. Sign it only when you are ready.

  4. Get started

    Fill out your first set of surveys. Our team is here to help along the way.

Common questions

How much time does it take?

In the Registry, the first set of surveys takes about 30 to 45 minutes. After that, monthly surveys take about 5 to 10 minutes. In the Deep Phenotyping Cohort, the first visit takes place over two days. There is one shorter follow-up visit about a year later.

Will I be paid?

Each quarter, we hold a drawing for Registry participants. Some people win a $50 gift card. Deep Phenotyping participants receive $500 for the first visit and $150 for the follow-up visit. People who choose to give an optional tissue sample receive $125.

Is my information private?

Your study data and samples are labeled with a study code, not your name. Your name and address are not stored with them. Only approved researchers can see the data, and they cannot download it. Any use of the data must be approved by HeartShare leaders.

Can I stop taking part?

Yes. You can stop at any time, for any reason. Stopping will not affect your regular medical care. To stop, contact the study team.

Will I get my test results?

Deep Phenotyping participants get results from tests that may matter for their health. Registry participants do not get individual results. Everyone can see updates about what we are learning on this website.
