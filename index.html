How participation works

Everyone starts in the Registry. Some people in the Registry may then be able to join the Deep Phenotyping Cohort.

01

The HeartShare Registry

After you agree to join, you will fill out online health surveys. The first set takes about 30 to 45 minutes. After that, monthly surveys take about 5 to 10 minutes.

What Registry participation involves
02

Deep Phenotyping Cohort

Some Registry participants will be eligible for this in-person study. It starts with a detailed health exam over two days. About one year later, there is a shorter follow-up visit. More visits may be added in the future. The study team will go over the schedule and any optional tests with you.

Learn about study visits

Privacy, explained in practical terms.

Before you choose to participate, you deserve specific information about how we handle data, who may access it, and what choices remain yours. These points reflect the current study protocol.

Learn more about privacy and data sharing
Study identifiers

Data and biospecimens collected through study evaluations use study identification codes. Identifying information like names and addresses are not stored with the data and biospecimens.

Secure research access

Only de-identified data and images can be uploaded to NHLBI BioData Catalyst, a cloud platform for research use only. Access is limited to researchers authorized by the HeartShare Data Translation Center, and data and images cannot be downloaded.

Review of use

Use of study data, biospecimens, and resulting findings is subject to HeartShare Steering Committee approval and must follow guidelines set by the Publications and Ancillary Studies Committees.
